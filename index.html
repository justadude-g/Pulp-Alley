Leader/Sidekick/Ally/Follower/Gang auto-set Level (and Health, below) from the Core Rules p. 8-9 table when you pick Card Type — you can still change it after. Level 4 is the rulebook maximum.

Non-Player Characters (advanced rules): NPCs are Passive (resting, patrolling, or wandering — limited movement, can't attack) until an enemy comes within 12" or a nearby character attacks or is attacked, then they become Alert (Brawler, Shooter, or Scout) for the rest of the scenario. NPCs don't normally perform actions, except via an ability that includes one (e.g. Occult), while driving a vehicle, or when a scenario allows it. The Ability Library's "NPC only" abilities (Crush, Evasive, Occult, Torment, Toxin, Vicious) are built for this role.

Gangs never roll Health checks. Casualties are removed one model at a time; the gang is knocked out at 2 models or fewer (p. 21). Set "Current Models" above.

Errata — Gangs & Horror: Gangs roll 1d6 for Horror checks, and may roll 1d6 to Recover from Horror cards/effects.
